\chapter{Success criteria and how they were judged}
\label{app:criteria}

Each criterion below carries a threshold and the reason that threshold was
chosen. A criterion with a number attached but no reason for the number is not
a criterion.

\begin{table}[H]
  \centering
  \small
  \caption[Success criteria]{The nine criteria this work is judged against.}
  \label{tab:criteria}
  \begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.04\linewidth}p{0.26\linewidth}p{0.36\linewidth}p{0.24\linewidth}@{}}
    \toprule
    & \textbf{Criterion} & \textbf{Threshold, and why} & \textbf{Outcome} \\
    \midrule
    1 & Angle conversion is exact & Converting an angle from the
      manufacturer's convention to the software's and back must return the
      original. Any deviation is a wrapping mistake, and the failure mode is
      nearly a full turn of a joint beside a person & Met \\
    2 & Commands go where intended & Within \SI{30}{\degree} per direction.
      Beyond that the driver has to consciously correct rather than move
      naturally & Partly met: up, down, forward and back pass; sideways does
      not \\
    3 & The arm follows the command in simulation & Within
      \SI{5}{\milli\metre}. Below that the residual is the deadband rather
      than the controller & Met, \SIrange{0.7}{4.7}{\milli\metre} \\
    4 & Distance to the wearer & At least \SI{150}{\milli\metre} at all times
      & Not met on three of the four tasks, reported per task \\
    5 & Every injected fault is caught & All of them. A mechanism that catches
      thirteen of fourteen is not a safety mechanism & Met, 14 of 14 \\
    6 & Failed channels inject nothing & Below \SI{1}{\milli\metre} of
      commanded movement from a still hand, and a refusal when nothing usable
      remains & Partly met: one arm passes at
      \SI{0.014}{\milli\metre}, the other fails at \SI{54.5}{\milli\metre} \\
    7 & Every declared position is reachable & Ten repeats out of ten, along
      the whole path. A single successful attempt is a coin flip & Met, 17 of
      17 \\
    8 & End-to-end delay & Under \SI{200}{\milli\second} & Not measured. The
      highest-value remaining gap \\
    9 & Grasps are verifiable in the recordings & The fingers must open, close
      on the object and reopen, and the object must move with the hand & Met,
      all recorded grasps \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Two criteria are deliberately absent. There is no force or stiffness criterion,
because the simulator returns exactly zero for every force reading, so any
quantity derived from one is undefined on this platform rather than merely
unmeasured. And there is no wrist-tracking criterion, because the wrist
orientation is pinned and the channels that would command it are the dead
ones.
