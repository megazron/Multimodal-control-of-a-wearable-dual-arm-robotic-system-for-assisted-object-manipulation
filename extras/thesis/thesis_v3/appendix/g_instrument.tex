\chapter{Measurement validity: the full record}
\label{app:instrument}
\label{ch:instrument}

\noindent\emph{\Cref{ch:discussionmain} states the validity argument and the
two numbers that carry it. This appendix is the evidence: what each of the
twenty-six cases actually was, grouped by mechanism.}

\begin{quote}
\textbf{A surprising failure is evidence about the instrument until the
instrument has been cleared.}
\end{quote}

Twenty-six times a measurement in this project was wrong and the system was
not. In every case the wrong reading was \emph{plausible} -- none announced
itself -- and two would have removed results now central to this thesis.
Sorted by mechanism, they fall into five groups.

\begin{description}
  \item[An absence read as a value (7 cases).] Zero variance from
    oversampling a slower sensor (\cref{sec:channelhealth}); a detection
    rate of \SIrange{0}{4}{\percent} from a renderer producing images
    unlike anything the detector was trained on; a surface height reporting
    the fitted plane's intercept at the origin rather than over the working
    area.
  \item[A test harness constructing the condition it tests (6 cases).] A
    reachability control comparing a search-cap lower bound against a
    boundary, which was never going to pass regardless of the arm's true
    reach; a path-verification failure from the verifier's own test path
    running in the one direction that leaves the feasible region.
  \item[State left over from a previous run (5 cases).] A reachability
    sweep measured with the arms parked \SI{0.87}{} and \SI{0.59}{\radian}
    from home, reporting zero failures under the same name as the
    genuine measurement.
  \item[Order of operations inside the measurement (5 cases).] An isotropy
    statistic taken as the minimum over directions of a single sweep,
    dominated by rare early solver terminations and varying by a factor of
    two between runs; stable once repeated ten times and read as a median.
  \item[A constant asserted in more than one place, measured in none (3
    cases).] The scene camera's mounting orientation
    (\cref{sec:orientation}); the wrist-to-pad offset, treated as a scalar
    when it is a function of gripper opening.
\end{description}

Two numbers carry the argument. \textbf{Fifteen of the twenty-six were
caught by an independently known quantity contradicting the reading} --
five by inspecting the code, six by looking at the raw artefact, usually an
image. Care was not the defence that worked; redundancy was.
\textbf{Fourteen of the twenty-six were already answerable from something in
hand} -- a published stroke, an arithmetic prediction, a quantity exact by
construction -- which is the recommendation this project's experience
supports: build the cross-check in rather than trying harder.

\section{What follows from it}

Every reachability figure in this report carries its repeat count, because
one solver call is one coin flip. Every stage of the simulation campaign
carries controls that must \emph{fail}. Where a quantity has never been
measured, the text says so rather than offering an estimate -- the camera
mounting transform in the positioning budget is one such term, and it is not
small (\cref{sec:hardware-session}). Any unreproduced single measurement in
this report should be read as provisional.

\section{Three standing caveats}

\textbf{Simulation is not the arm.} Every quantitative result except the
channel measurements and the pilot comes from simulation with mock
hardware, whose force channels are identically zero -- any quantity derived
from force is structurally undefined, not merely unmeasured.
\textbf{Latency figures are lower bounds}, measured between software
stages, excluding the operator, the display and the physical arm's
response. \textbf{The one-stack constraint}: two instances of the master
node once split the serial stream between them and invalidated a full day
of measurements before the cause was found; this is now prevented at the
file-descriptor level, but any measurement taken before that prevention
carries a risk that cannot be retrospectively excluded.
