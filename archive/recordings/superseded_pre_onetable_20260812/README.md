# SUPERSEDED — every clip here predates the bench deletion

These directories were recorded against the TWO-LEVEL layout: a table at
z=0.95 and a raised "bench" at z=1.10 whose near edge sat at y=0.245.

**They are superseded because that layout was measured to be unusable**, not
because of anything wrong with the recordings themselves:

* the bench cost **0.250 m of forward reach** (no furniture 0.425, table only
  0.425, bench only 0.175);
* its top surface spanned y 0.245..0.630 while the reachable band was
  y 0.05..0.20 — an overlap of **exactly zero**, so no object could ever rest
  on it;
* all four T1 cubes were therefore over **no surface at all**, floating
  0.170 m above the table and 15–75 mm in front of the bench's near edge;
* cube_2 straddled BOTH coloured planes (10 x 40 mm each), so the cubes began
  heaped on their own targets.
