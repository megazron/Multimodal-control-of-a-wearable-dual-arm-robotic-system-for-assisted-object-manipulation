   between-subjects alternative needs 96 participants to reach the same
   sensitivity.};

\node[draw, fill=gray!4, text width=136mm, align=left, anchor=west] at (-16, -62)
  {\textbf{Why not all four modes.} Four modes $\times$ five tasks is
   \SIrange{260}{320}{\minute} of task time, a \SIrange{5.5}{6.5}{\hour}
   session, against a \SI{48}{\minute} cap on how long a person can wear
   \SI{17}{\kilo\gram}. The three costed options were:\\[3pt]
   \begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}
   \toprule
   option & conditions & session & detectable effect\\
   \midrule
   mode between subjects & 5 & \SI{127}{\minute} & only $f=0.40$, needs $n=96$\\
   all modes, one task & 8 & \SI{169}{\minute} & breaks the pack-on cap\\
   \textbf{two modes, five tasks} & \textbf{10} & \textbf{\SI{167}{\minute}}
     & \textbf{$d=0.73$ at 80\% power, $n=32$}\\
   \bottomrule
   \end{tabular}\\[3pt]
   Dropping VR and full autonomy is a real loss of scope and is recorded as
   one. The comparison kept is the one where the literature disagrees with
   itself.};
