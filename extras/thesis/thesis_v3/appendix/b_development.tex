\chapter{The route the design took}
\label{app:development}
\label{ch:development}

\noindent\emph{A record of process, not of result: the routes tried and
abandoned before \cref{ch:method} took its delivered form. Recovered prior
programs are archived at \repo{thesis\_report/\_source/prior\_work/}, dated
7 June to 27 July 2026; the full divergence analysis against the planning
report is at \repo{thesis\_report/DIVERGENCE.md}.}

The first master-to-slave mapping sent master joint angles directly to slave
joint angles. It failed for a structural reason rather than a tuning one:
forward kinematics compounds every joint angle in the chain, so a
\SI{9}{\milli\metre}/\SI{3}{\degree} disagreement between the master's CAD
and its measured link lengths (\cref{sec:cad-not-source}) propagates to a
materially wrong slave pose at the wrist, and there is no reconstruction
path once a channel carrying an intermediate joint fails. Task-space
mapping, sending master \emph{tip displacement} to slave end-effector
displacement, decouples the two chains and is what shipped -- at the cost of
leaving the slave's redundant degree of freedom unspecified, which is what
\cref{ch:workspace} later shows to be expensive near the wearer.

Two further redirections are worth recording because each was forced by a
measurement rather than chosen for elegance. The planning report's haptic
objective -- physical springs at the master, virtual impedance at the slave
-- was abandoned once the arm's cyclic control interface was found unusable
over the available network path (\cref{ch:resultsmain}): an impedance law
running at \SI{18}{\hertz} is not an impedance law. And validating the
master-slave mapping in simulation before touching hardware, as planned,
used MuJoCo~\cite{todorov2012mujoco} rather than Isaac
Lab~\cite{isaaclab} -- it starts in under a second, its model format is one
readable file, and for a kinematic-mapping question its contact solver is
irrelevant, so the heavier alternative bought nothing.

Sensor faults turned out to be the dominant failure mode, not control
faults: injecting a stuck analog reading sent a wrong angle to the
corresponding slave joint with no reconstruction available under the
joint-space mapping, which is the finding that motivated the graceful
degradation architecture of \cref{ch:method} and, with it, the reduced
azimuth/elevation/reach decomposition that the task-space mapping made
possible.
